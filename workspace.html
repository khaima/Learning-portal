School list

Every county and school dropdown in the portal — sign-up, field visits, user editing and the filters above — uses this list, and updates live when you change it. Each school gets a code, and every teacher, school head and learner placed in it gets their own code under it (e.g. NRK-001-T01).

Manage counties

The code (2–4 letters) starts every school code in that county, e.g. SMB-001, and can't be changed later. A county with no schools can be removed from its section below.

Staff accounts

Every staff sign-in, grouped by role. New accounts wait here for approval; administrators can invite staff (by email, or a link to copy), change roles and schools, and suspend, deactivate or reactivate accounts. Nobody can see anyone's password — "Reset password" emails a reset link, or makes a one-time temporary password they must replace when they first sign in.

They get a link to create their account with exactly this role and placement — emailed by the portal, or copy it and send it yourself (WhatsApp, SMS…). It works once and expires in 14 days.

Email sending isn't set up for the portal yet, so copy the link and send it yourself.
