M&E reports

A report freezes the results for a programme, period and place (the county / school filter at the top). A draft can be refreshed; once final it never changes.

School list

Every county and school dropdown in the portal — sign-up, field visits, user editing and the filters above — uses this list, and updates live when you change it. Each school gets a code, and every teacher, school head and learner placed in it gets their own code under it (e.g. NRK-001-T01).

Manage counties

The code (2–4 letters) starts every school code in that county, e.g. SMB-001, and can't be changed later. A county with no schools can be removed from its section below.

Staff accounts

Every staff sign-in, grouped by role. New accounts wait here for approval; administrators can invite staff, change roles and schools, and suspend, deactivate or reactivate accounts. Passwords are one-way hashed and can't be viewed by anyone — including this dashboard — so "Set new password" sets a brand-new one instead of showing the old one.

The person gets a link to create their account with exactly this role and placement. It works once and expires in 14 days. No email is sent — copy the link and send it to them yourself.

    Field surveys (KoboToolbox)

    Connect the KoboToolbox account that hosts your field surveys. Each survey must include a hidden question whose data column name is officer_ref — the portal fills it with the field officer's ID so submissions match back automatically.

    View shows the actual questions right here for a look — nothing is sent to field officers. Attach is what puts it on every field officer's dashboard.
